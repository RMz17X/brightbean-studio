Privacy Policy

Operator: Hijitoko (“we”, “us”). Service: Hijitoko Studio — a self-hosted social media publishing tool (based on BrightBean Studio) used by channel owners to publish and schedule their own videos and posts. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

This service is a tool for channel owners. It is not a public social network, and it does not publish anything on its own initiative. Every post is created, scheduled, or published only on the instruction of the owner of the channel concerned.

1. What data we access

When a channel owner connects an account, we access only what is needed to publish and manage that owner’s own content:

  • YouTube (Google): via the YouTube Data API, scope youtube.upload — to upload videos to the owner’s own channel and read the upload result. We do not read the owner’s private watch history, subscriptions, or comments beyond what is needed to complete a publish.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): via the Meta Graph API — to publish videos, Reels, and posts to the owner’s own Facebook Page and linked Instagram Professional (Business or Creator) account, and to read publishing status and basic insights for those posts.
  • TikTok: via the TikTok Content Posting API — to upload and publish videos to the owner’s own TikTok account, on the owner’s instruction.
  • Account and workspace data: email address, channel/account identifiers, post titles, captions, schedules, and uploaded media files, as entered by the channel owner in the tool.

2. How we use the data

We use the data solely to provide the publishing service: preparing, scheduling, uploading, and reporting the status of the channel owner’s own posts, and showing the owner their own publishing history and analytics. We do not use Google, Meta, or TikTok user data to train models, to profile users, or for any purpose unrelated to the publishing task the owner asked for.

3. What we do NOT do with your data

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not use Google, Meta, or TikTok user data for third-party advertising, and we do not transfer it to data brokers.
  • We do not publish to any account you have not connected, and we do not contact your audience.

4. How data is stored and protected

Access tokens and platform credentials are stored encrypted in our own database on our own server, and are used only to call the platform APIs on the owner’s behalf. Uploaded media is stored on our own server and is fetched by the platforms only when publishing a post the owner created. We do not store platform passwords.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep publishing records for as long as the owner’s workspace exists, so the owner can audit what was published and when. An owner may ask us to delete their workspace data by contacting us (see §7). Deleting data here does not delete posts already published on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok — those must be removed on the platform itself.

6. How to revoke access

You can revoke our access at any time, without contacting us:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → remove Hijitoko Studio.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Meta account settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites → remove the app.
  • TikTok: TikTok settings → Manage apps / Authorized apps → remove the app.

Revoking access stops future publishing immediately. Already published posts are unaffected.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@hijitoko.com (Hijitoko). If any part of this page changes, the “Last updated” date above will change.
